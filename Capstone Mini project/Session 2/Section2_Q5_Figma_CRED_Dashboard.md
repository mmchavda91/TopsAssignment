## CRED Design Language Applied:

- **Dark premium background** — `#0A0A0A` near black
- **Gold accents** — Score, coin count, card borders glow in gold
- **Purple neon glow** — Active rewards and highlighted cards
- **Locked/Unlocked system** — Gamification to drive engagement
- **Glassmorphism** — Frosted glass effect on all reward cards
